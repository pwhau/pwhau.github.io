# About Me
Started ordinary, sometimes envying others’ smooth paths.  
Curiosity kept me going, fascinated by different cultures, human experiences, and landscapes.  

I love art and learning —— photography, UI/UX, psychology, all self-taught explorations.  
I enjoy traveling with a Polaroid, captivated by frozen moments.  

Personality tests say I am a contradictory mix: introverted yet social, a workaholic yet longing for freedom.  
I need solitude but also crave sharing.  
Even if sometimes invisible, I still hope to leave my voice through writing or podcasts.

###### Venus in Pisces

# I Am a Traveler
Always searching for a home.  
Some say “home is where the heart is,” but environment matters to me.  
A suitcase is the starting point of home.  

Friends and family are support and memory.  
This site/Podcast is my "PPT" to rediscover myself and share with the world.  

Being a traveler is complex. Some you remember forget you; some you forget remember you.  
Experiences accumulate, and resonance with others often stays in the past.  
Long-awaited reunions, wanting to share everything, yet cannot access others’ memories like in *Dune*.  

I like to stay flexible.  
If paths diverge, it’s simply another chance to start again.  

Regarding family, I know I am loved, and perhaps that’s enough.
