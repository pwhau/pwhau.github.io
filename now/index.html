Now

Last Updated: September 27, 2025

🌱 Early spring brings fresh green leaves to the trees.

📍 Whanganui, New Zealand – a small town that even many locals rarely visit.

We moved here from Wellington in July. Our new home sits right on the riverbank, with stunning views.

From our balcony, we can watch a large tree transform from bare branches into a canopy of green.

I’m looking forward to life here, with less traffic noise and more warm sunlight filtering through the leaves.

September 2025: A New Project

Most of the episodes are in Mandarin for now—it’s simply the language I feel most natural expressing myself in. I’m considering translating it into English, but I’ll need some time to think about that.

This month, I started my solo podcast, “Tell wade why?”

At first, I wanted to share my travel stories, filled with fun and laughter.

I also wanted to talk about my wild ideas—surely I’m not the only one with thoughts like these?

And of course, I wanted to share heartfelt stories that might bring tears, because life is full of ups and downs.

But I’ve typed for so long that I’ve almost forgotten how to write by hand—or even how to speak naturally. The good thing is, I can always relearn.

In the end, I’m still doing what I find interesting, just on my own. Looking back, it was always my choice.

There may be less shared laughter and fewer connections, but we have our own little world.

Don’t worry, my friend—I’m doing quite well. How about you?

Maybe you’ll stumble across this message in a bottle someday.

By the way, it’s now a little tree, with a time capsule buried beneath it, hoping it will grow strong.

And if you’d like, we can share secrets in the tree hole — our secret hideout.

Hoping to connect with someone.

Starting Over

Every time I move to a new city, it feels like starting from scratch.

Is this what it’s like for kids who move and change schools often? But for adults, starting over always feels like a sunk cost.

Still, I thank myself for being brave and resilient. And I thank you—my friends—for being around.

Because even if no one is ever truly alone, I can still feel a glimmer of your presence in some lonely corner.

Yes, I’ve said all this just to admit: I’m job-hunting again. (Feel free to reach out!)

Sometimes I blame it on my INFP personality—this flawed, idealistic type that struggles in the “real world.”

Bending to the demands of earning a living, folding and unfolding, gently folding my dreams as well.

Taking Stock

I’ve picked up my pen again. I still love writing. Maybe I could become a travel writer?

I created this website back in 2020. Message me if you’re curious.

I’ve changed the theme once, but this is the original design

And yes—I actually added translations in eight languages functions for posts. (Like being invaded by eight countries at once?)

Is that a skill? Why keep this website at all? Because I don’t want to be controlled by a platform.

Current Goals
